## Pre-university education

I have developed extracurricular computing programmes for learners at different school levels:

- **Primary school:** [[teaching/primary/cikk-cakk-katica|Cikk-Cakk Katica]] — age-specific Minecraft-based activities for grades 5–6 and 7–8, introducing analytical thinking, planning, logic circuits and automation.
- **Secondary school:** [[teaching/secondary/cikk-cakk-katica|Cikk-Cakk Katica]] — a 25-topic curriculum organised around implementing a board game in Minecraft.
- **Secondary school:** [[teaching/secondary/python-graphs-programming|Python, Graphs and Programming]] — two-hour, four-hour and semester-long plans and practical materials centred on a movie recommendation system.

The linked programme descriptions retain concrete curriculum examples and distinguish educational plans from documented teaching assignments.

My broader [[artes-meae/ars-paedagogiae|educational philosophy]] connects these activities through learning by doing and mentoring.
