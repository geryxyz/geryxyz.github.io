title: Student Consultations
---

Students taking my courses can arrange a personal consultation with me.

## Arranging an appointment

Please [contact me by email](mailto:geryxyz@inf.u-szeged.hu) to arrange a mutually suitable time. In your request, specify:

- The course, thesis or internship concerned.
- The topic you would like to discuss.
- A few time slots when you are available.

If several students wish to discuss the same topic, only one of you needs to arrange the appointment. Please include the names of the other participants.

Course-related consultations always take place in person.

## Thesis consultations

The same consultation rules apply to BSc and MSc theses and internships. The initial meeting must always take place in person and be arranged in advance. Subsequent consultations may take place in person or online via Discord. In either case, consultations are spoken discussions.

## Location

All in-person consultations take place at the Department of Software Engineering, University of Szeged, Árpád tér 2, office 13.
