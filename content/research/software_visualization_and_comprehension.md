I investigate ways to make complex software systems easier to explore and understand through interactive visual representations. My [CodeMetropolis](https://codemetropolis.github.io/CodeMetropolis/) work uses the Minecraft game engine and a city metaphor to visualize source-code structure and testing metrics, including integration into everyday development environments.

A related direction is [[research/spin-off/software_sensing|Software Sensing]], which explores communicating code properties through sound and haptic feedback in addition to visual representations.
