Conlanging is the creation of constructed languages, or *conlangs*. Examples include Esperanto, Dothraki, Lojban, and Klingon.

I am particularly interested in the creative side of language construction. **Artistic languages**, or *artlangs*, can be designed for aesthetic and phonetic pleasure rather than practical usefulness. Constructing a language is a deliberate form of craftsmanship and creative expression.

This interest also appears in [[artes-meae/index|Artes Meae]], where I reflect on the relationship between language, communication, and creation. See also [[research/spin-off/theoretical_linguistics|Theoretical Linguistics]].
