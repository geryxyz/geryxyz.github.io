I explore how measurements, historical data, machine learning, and expert knowledge can inform software engineering decisions. My work covers development-effort prediction, developer productivity measurement, static-analysis actionability, and expert-guided analysis of large data structures.

This theme overlaps with the other research areas: data and models are useful when they help engineers make better decisions.
