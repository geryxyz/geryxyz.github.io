My research explores the role of developers, their behavior, experience, knowledge, and feedback in software engineering. I study how development activities can be observed and understood, and how human input can make automated tools more useful in practice.

Related work includes developer interaction data, micro-productivity profiles, and human-in-the-loop debugging.
